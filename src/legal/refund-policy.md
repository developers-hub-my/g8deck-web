This policy is part of the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms). It is short because the rule is simple: **monthly plans are not refunded for time you did not use; charges we made in error are refunded in full.**

## 1. Self-serve monthly plans

Starter, Growth and Business are billed monthly, in advance, in US dollars.

- **No refunds for partial months.** When you cancel or downgrade, the current period runs to its end and you keep full access until then. The unused part of the month is not refunded and is not credited.
- **Cancel at any time** from your Billing page in the console. Cancellation stops the next renewal; it does not cut off the period you have already paid for.
- **Downgrades** take effect at the end of the current period, with no proration.
- **Upgrades** take effect immediately and are billed as a full new period from the date of payment.

## 2. Trial

The trial is **free and needs no card**. Nothing is charged until you choose a paid plan, so there is nothing to refund. When the trial ends without a paid plan, the organisation simply cannot create anything new; nothing already running is stopped and nothing is deleted.

## 3. Charges made in error

We refund **in full** any charge that should not have happened:

- a **duplicate charge** for the same invoice;
- a charge made **after you had cancelled** and the paid period had ended;
- a charge for the **wrong amount** or against the wrong plan.

The refund goes back **to the card you paid with, through Stripe** — never to a different card or by bank transfer — and we issue a **credit note** against the original invoice so your records and ours match. We cannot refund part of an invoice through Stripe; where only part of a charge was wrong we issue a partial credit note and settle it against your next invoice or by another agreed method.

## 4. Annual licences — self-hosted, Academic and Enterprise

Annual licences are sold under a **signed order**, and the order governs. Where the order is silent, the default is that a licence fee is **not refundable** once the term has started. We may, at our discretion, agree to credit the unused part of a term pro rata by credit note — for example where an institution's cohort ends early — but that is a decision made on request, not an entitlement.

## 5. How to ask

Email [hello@devhub.my](mailto:hello@devhub.my) from the address on your account with:

1. the **invoice number** (it is on the invoice email and on your Billing page);
2. what you believe went wrong;
3. anything that helps us check it — the date of the charge, or the receipt Stripe emailed you.

We acknowledge every request and, where a refund is due, **issue it within 14 days** of agreeing it. Stripe then returns the money to your card; how long that takes to appear on your statement depends on your card issuer, and is usually 5 to 10 business days.

## 6. Disputes

If you disagree with our decision, reply and say so — a second person will look at it. Please do this before opening a chargeback with your card issuer: a chargeback takes weeks to resolve and costs both of us a fee, and we can usually settle the matter in days. Nothing in this policy limits any right you have under Malaysian consumer protection law.
