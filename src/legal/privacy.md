## 4. Where your data is kept, and transfers abroad

The G8Deck control plane and its database run in **DigitalOcean's Singapore region**. Your applications, their databases and their files run on **your own servers**, wherever you put them; G8Deck records their addresses and credentials but does not copy your workload data to the control plane.

Because Singapore is outside Malaysia, and because Stripe, Cloudflare, SparkPost and Netlify operate internationally, personal data is **transferred outside Malaysia**. By using the service you consent to that transfer for the purposes in section 2. Each processor is bound by contract to protect the data to a standard at least equivalent to what the Act requires. A self-hosted installation stores everything in section 1 on your own infrastructure, and none of it reaches us unless you send it to support.

## 5. How we protect it

- **Encryption in transit** — every connection to the console and this site is over HTTPS.
- **Encryption at rest for credentials** — server credentials, provider tokens, application secrets and our own payment-processor keys are encrypted before they are written to the database. Server credentials can be revealed only by the organisation's owner and administrators; our payment-processor keys are never shown back at all.
- **Passwords** are stored as one-way hashes. **Two-factor authentication** and **passkeys** are available on every account; we recommend enabling one of them.
- **An audit trail** records every significant action with the actor, the time and the source, and the governance audit log is append-only — it cannot be edited or deleted through the application.
- **Access controls** — roles at organisation and team level limit who can see and change what, and the same rules are enforced through the console, the API and the automation servers.
- **Private database endpoint** — the control plane's database is reachable only from the control plane, over TLS.

No system is perfectly secure. If we discover a breach that affects your personal data we will tell you without undue delay, tell you what happened and what we are doing about it, and notify the authorities where the law requires.

## 7. Your rights

Under the Act you have the right to:

- **Access** the personal data we hold about you and be told how it is used;
- **Correct** it where it is inaccurate, incomplete, misleading or out of date;
- **Withdraw consent** to processing, subject to the notice that our contract with you requires and to the records we must keep by law;
- **Limit** processing that is likely to cause you distress;
- **Object** to processing for direct marketing (we do none, but the right exists);
- **Complain** to us, and to the Personal Data Protection Commissioner of Malaysia if you are not satisfied with our answer.

Most of these you can do yourself: your name, email, password and security settings are in **Settings › Profile** in the console, and the billing profile is on the organisation's Billing page. For anything else, email [hello@devhub.my](mailto:hello@devhub.my) from the address on your account with the subject **"PDPA request"** and tell us what you want. We will confirm your identity, answer within **21 days**, and tell you if we need longer or if the law prevents us from doing what you ask. We do not charge for a request unless it is manifestly excessive, and we will tell you before charging.

## 8. Cookies

The console sets **one session cookie**, which is needed to keep you signed in, and the cookies its security framework needs to protect forms from forgery. They are not used to track you and they expire when the session does. This website sets no cookies at all.

The console can be configured to load Google Analytics or Google Tag Manager. **At the effective date of this notice neither is configured**, and no analytics script is served. If that changes we will update this notice and the version number before it takes effect.

## 9. Children

The service is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18 except where an educational institution has opened an organisation for its students and holds the consent it needs; in that case the institution is responsible for that consent. If you believe a child has given us data outside such an arrangement, email us and we will remove it.
